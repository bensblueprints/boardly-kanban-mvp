Do I need a credit card to get started?+

No. Basic Free includes one company, one user and 2 GB of storage. Start without entering payment details.

How does AI work in Boardly?+

Connect your AI provider in account settings to use supported project AI features. Your provider’s usage is separate from the free workspace. Company-wide orchestration and native Codex cloud workers are currently in private preview.

Can I use Boardly with a team?+

Company and project membership supports Editor and Viewer permissions. Basic Free includes one user. Additional seats and larger account plans require an upgrade when paid billing becomes available.

Does the desktop experience work offline?+

The current desktop experience opens your cloud workspace in a dedicated browser app window. It requires internet access; offline sync with the legacy native app is not available.

When can I rent a computer?+

Hardware setup is underway. Check ComputerUse.space for availability. Rentals open after readiness checks, and desktop control is awaiting the worker desktop service.
