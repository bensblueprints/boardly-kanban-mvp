**PRESS RELEASE — DRAFT**

**Introducing Boardly: Companies, Client Projects and AI Agents. One Intelligent Workspace.**

*Boardly is building the next generation of kanban: a place to organize a business, direct its AI agents and see what needs attention.*

**September 7, 2026** — Boardly today introduces its vision for the most intelligent kanban board on the planet: a connected workspace where entrepreneurs and agencies can manage their companies, client projects, tasks and AI agents in one place.

Running several businesses or serving multiple clients means keeping track of more than deadlines. Every project has its own conversations, files, decisions and dependencies. As AI agents take on more work, business owners also need to know what those agents are doing, where they are working and when they need help.

Boardly brings that activity into the same workspace as the work itself.

Companies contain boards. Boards contain projects. Projects hold tasks, conversations, files and the context agents need to carry out their assignments. Users can work with an individual project agent or create a swarm of agents across selected projects within a board or company.

An animated company dashboard connects those pieces visually. Users can see active agents, inspect their current work and review blockers across the company. Saved conversations and activity histories keep progress available when users return.

Boardly also gives users a clear choice about how they want to engage with AI:

- **Ask:** Explore an idea, understand a project or get clarification.
- **Plan:** Discuss an approach and work through the steps before taking action.
- **Work:** Authorize agents to carry out an assignment and report their progress.

Ask and Plan keep the conversation free of execution, giving users room to think before committing to a course of action. Work connects that decision to the project where it belongs.

For technical projects, Boardly supports saved, encrypted SSH connections with company or project permissions, allowing authorized Work agents to access the servers they need.

Cloud-running agents keep assigned work moving independently of a user's laptop. Agents continue across working sessions until they finish, encounter a blocker or are stopped. When human input is needed, Boardly records the blocker and the next action, so users can supply the missing information and resume the same assignment. Saved progress also supports recovery when the worker restarts.

Account-level Tailscale connections let users connect Boardly to their private devices through a separate sign-in. With authorized SSH access, Work agents can reach selected servers while company and project permissions determine which connections they may use.

Boardly's ambition is straightforward: give business owners one place to direct work, understand progress and decide what happens next.

Boardly is currently in private preview. Public availability will be announced separately.

**About Boardly**

Boardly is an intelligent kanban workspace for managing companies, client projects and AI agents. It combines visual project organization, contextual AI conversations, agent swarms and company-wide activity in one connected application.
